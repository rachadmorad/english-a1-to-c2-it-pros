# How to use this repo / كيف تستخدم هذا المستودع

## Your weekly routine (5 sessions × 45–60 min ≈ 5 hours per week)
| Session | What to do |
|---|---|
| **1 — Learn** | Vocabulary table (say each word 3×) + read the **Lesson** slowly. Use the Arabic notes. |
| **2 — Practice** | Do all the exercises **on paper**. Then check the answer key. Correct mistakes in red. |
| **3 — Listen** | Dialogue: listen only → listen + read → shadow (repeat right after the speaker). Add 10 min of free audio (`resources/audio-resources.md`). |
| **4 — Speak & write** | The Speak/Write tasks. **Record your voice** on your phone and listen again. Play the game. |
| **5 — Test** | Mini-quiz (target 7/8). Re-do the exercises you got wrong. Review the flashcards. |

**Every day (10 minutes):** Anki flashcards. Short daily contact is better than a long weekly session.

## The 5 rules of good learning
1. **Speak out loud.** Reading silently doesn't make you speak.
2. **Learn chunks**, not single words: *"Can you repeat that, please?"* not just *repeat*.
3. **Mistakes are data.** Keep a "My mistakes" notebook with the wrong and the right sentence.
4. **Repeat old units.** Every 4th session, redo one older exercise.
5. **Use English in your work now:** label your desk items, write your notes and tickets in English (even 3 words).

## Arabic and English — helpful differences
| Arabic | English |
|---|---|
| No verb "to be" in the present | You **must** say am / is / are |
| Adjective after noun (لابتوب جديد) | Adjective **before** noun (**new laptop**) |
| No capital letters | Capitals for names, days, months, "I" |
| Written right to left | Left to right — practice writing on lined paper |
| Verb first (VSO) is common | English word order is fixed: **Subject – Verb – Object** |

## Pass marks
Mini-quiz 7/8 · Progress test 80% · Final exam 80%. If you are under the pass mark, repeat the units shown next to each wrong answer.
