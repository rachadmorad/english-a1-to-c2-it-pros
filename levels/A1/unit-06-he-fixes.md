## 2. Lesson / الدرس

### 1. he / she / it + verb-s
| Rule | Base → he/she/it |
|---|---|
| most verbs: + **s** | work → work**s**, check → check**s** |
| ends in s, sh, ch, x, o: + **es** | fix → fix**es**, watch → watch**es**, go → go**es** |
| consonant + y → **ies** | study → stud**ies**, try → tr**ies** |
| irregular | have → **has**, do → **does** |

**Examples:** Sara **fixes** printers. The firewall **blocks** attacks. Omar **has** a server room.

### 2. Negative: doesn't + BASE verb
Sara **doesn't work** on Friday. (**not** ~~doesn't works~~) — After *doesn't* the verb has **no s**.

### 3. Questions: Does + subject + BASE verb?
| Question | Short answer |
|---|---|
| **Does** he work here? | Yes, he **does**. / No, he **doesn't**. |
| **Does** the antivirus scan files? | Yes, it **does**. |
| **What does** she do? | She's a network engineer. / She fixes routers. |

### 4. Compare
| I / you / we / they | he / she / it |
|---|---|
| I work | She work**s** |
| I don't work | She **doesn't** work |
| Do you work? | **Does** she work? |

### 5. Pronunciation of -s
/s/ works, checks, blocks · /z/ installs, updates · /ɪz/ fixes, watches.

## 3. Watch out! Common mistakes for Arabic speakers / انتبه

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right | Why / لماذا |
|---|---|---|
| She work here. | **She works here.** | لا تنسَ s مع he/she/it. |
| He doesn't works. | **He doesn't work.** | بعد doesn't نستخدم الفعل الأصلي. |
| Does he works? | **Does he work?** | بعد Does الفعل الأصلي. |
| He have a server. | **He has a server.** | have → has. |

## 4. 🎧 Dialogue — Who does what?

**Sara:** Omar, what does Ali do?  
**Omar:** He manages the team. He doesn't fix computers.  
**Sara:** Does he answer calls?  
**Omar:** No, he doesn't. Nadia answers calls. She works at the help desk.  
**Sara:** And who monitors the network?  
**Omar:** I do. I monitor it every day.  
**Sara:** Does the firewall block spam?  
**Omar:** Yes, it does. It blocks a lot of spam.  

🇸🇦 *سارة تسأل عمر عن مهام كل شخص في الفريق: علي يدير، ناديا ترد على المكالمات، وعمر يراقب الشبكة.*

**How to use it:** (1) Listen without reading. (2) Listen and read. (3) Read aloud with a partner (shadowing). (4) Cover one side and say your lines from memory.

## 6. Speak & Write / تحدّث واكتب

**Speak:** Describe a colleague or family member: *He/She works ... He/She doesn't ... He/She has ...* (5 sentences).

**Write:** *A day in the life of an IT technician* (6 sentences with he/she).

## 7. Game / لعبة

**Game — Job Guess:** Write 6 jobs (*network engineer, help desk agent, security analyst, manager, technician, system administrator*) on cards. Pick one. Friend asks *Does he fix printers? Does she monitor the network?* until he guesses.
