# Unit 1 — Hello! I'm an IT technician
### مرحبًا! أنا فني تقنية معلومات

> ⏱️ Time: 5–6 study sessions of 45 minutes  |  🎧 Audio script: `audio-scripts/unit-01.txt`

## 🎯 Goals / الأهداف
- Greet people and say goodbye
- Introduce yourself: name, job, country
- Use I am / you are / he is / she is
- Spell names and emails with the alphabet

## 2. Lesson / الدرس

### 1. Greetings / التحيات
| When | Say |
|---|---|
| Morning (until 12:00) | **Good morning** |
| Afternoon (12:00 – 6:00) | **Good afternoon** |
| Evening (after 6:00) | **Good evening** |
| Meeting someone | **Hello!** / **Hi!** (informal) |
| Leaving | **Goodbye.** / **Bye!** / **See you tomorrow.** |

> "Good night" is only for going to sleep or leaving very late.

### 2. The verb *to be* (am / is / are) / الفعل "يكون"
In Arabic we say "أنا مهندس" — **no verb**. In English the verb is **always** needed: ~~I engineer~~ → **I am an engineer.**

| Pronoun | to be | Short form | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | **am** | I'm | I'm a technician. |
| you | **are** | you're | You're a user. |
| he | **is** | he's | He's a manager. |
| she | **is** | she's | She's an engineer. |

- **Negative:** add *not* → I'm **not** a manager. / He **isn't** a manager. / You **aren't** a user.
- **Question:** change the order → **Are** you an engineer? **Is** she a manager?
- **Short answers:** Yes, I **am**. / No, he **isn't**. (Don't use short forms in "Yes" answers: ~~Yes, I'm~~.)

### 3. a / an
- **a** before a consonant *sound*: **a** manager, **a** user
- **an** before a vowel *sound*: **an** engineer, **an** IT technician (IT starts with the sound "eye")

### 4. Say where you are from
**I'm from** Morocco. / **She's from** Egypt. / **Are you from** Jordan? — Use *from* + country.

### 5. Alphabet and spelling
Use the alphabet to spell names, usernames and emails. See `cheatsheets/pronunciation-guide.md`.
- `sara@corp.example` → "S-A-R-A **at** C-O-R-P **dot** E-X-A-M-P-L-E"
- Say **at** for @ and **dot** for the point.

## 3. Watch out! Common mistakes for Arabic speakers / انتبه

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right | Why / لماذا |
|---|---|---|
| I technician. | **I am a technician.** | لا نحذف الفعل am/is/are أبدًا. / Never drop the verb. |
| She a manager. | **She is a manager.** | نفس القاعدة مع she. |
| He are an engineer. | **He is an engineer.** | he ← is وليس are. |
| I am not manager. | **I'm not a manager.** | نحتاج a قبل المهنة. / Jobs need a / an. |

## 4. 🎧 Dialogue — A new colleague

**Sara:** Good morning! I'm Sara. I'm the new IT technician.  
**Omar:** Hello, Sara. I'm Omar. Nice to meet you.  
**Sara:** Nice to meet you, too. Are you the IT manager?  
**Omar:** No, I'm not. I'm a network engineer. Ali is the manager.  
**Sara:** Is Ali here?  
**Omar:** Yes, he is. He's at the help desk.  
**Sara:** Thank you, Omar!  
**Omar:** You're welcome.  

🇸🇦 *سارة فنية جديدة تلتقي بعمر (مهندس شبكات) وتسأله عن المدير علي، وهو في مكتب الدعم الفني.*

**How to use it:** (1) Listen without reading. (2) Listen and read. (3) Read aloud with a partner (shadowing). (4) Cover one side and say your lines from memory.

## 7. Game / لعبة

**Game — Introduction Bingo (with a friend or alone with the audio):** Draw a 3×3 grid and write nine words from the vocabulary. Listen to the dialogue; tick each word you hear. First to tick a full line says *"Bingo!"*

## 8. Mini-quiz / اختبار قصير (8 questions — target 7/8)

**1.** ___ Sara. I'm the new technician.  
a) I are  b) I am  c) I is

**2.** He ___ an IT manager.  
a) am  b) are  c) is

**3.** At 8:00 in the morning you say:  
a) Good evening  b) Good morning  c) Good night

**4.** Which is correct?  
a) She engineer.  b) She is engineer.  c) She is an engineer.

**5.** 'Is Ali here?' — short answer, yes:  
a) Yes, he's.  b) Yes, he is.  c) Yes, is.

**6.** 'Are you a manager?' — short answer, no:  
a) No, I not.  b) No, I amn't.  c) No, I'm not.

**7.** Choose a / an: ___ IT technician  
a) an  b) a  c) the

**8.** How do you say @ in an email?  
a) and  b) at  c) dot
