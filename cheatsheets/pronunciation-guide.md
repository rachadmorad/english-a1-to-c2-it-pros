## Difficult sounds for Arabic speakers
| Problem | Tip | Practice |
|---|---|---|
| **P vs B** — Arabic has no /p/ | Put your hand near your mouth. **P** = a puff of air. **B** = no air. | **p**ort / **b**ort, **p**ack / **b**ack, **p**rinter / **b**rinter |
| **V vs F** | **V**: teeth on lip + voice. **F**: same but no voice. | **v**ery / **f**ury, **v**irus / **f**irus |
| **Short vowels** /ɪ/ vs /iː/ | ship = short, sheep = long | it / eat, fill / feel |
| **th** | Good news: you have ث and ذ! **think** = ث, **this** = ذ | **th**ree, **th**at, **th**anks |
| **ng** | Don't say "n-g": *sing* /sɪŋ/ | sing, ring, working |
| **Consonant clusters** | Don't add a vowel at the start: **street** (not "istreet") | **st**op, **sc**an, **sp**am |
| **-s / -ed endings** | short and light: works /s/, installs /z/, fixes /ɪz/ | |

## Word stress / النبر
2-syllable nouns: stress the **first**: **LAP**top, **SER**ver, **PRINT**er.
Many verbs: stress the **second**: re**START**, up**DATE**, in**STALL**.
Numbers: thir**TEEN** (13) vs **THIR**ty (30).
