## 2. Lesson / الدرس

### 1. Wh- questions / أسئلة الاستفهام
| Word | Asks about | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **What** | things | **What** is your problem? |
| **Where** | place | **Where** are you? |
| **Who** | people | **Who** is your manager? |
| **When** | time | **When** does the error start? |
| **Why** | reason | **Why** is the network slow? |
| **How** | manner | **How** can I help you? |
| **How many** | number (countable) | **How many** users are there? |
| **Which** | choice | **Which** laptop is broken? |

### 2. Word order in questions
**Wh-word + do/does/is/are/can + subject + verb?**
- **What** do you see? (do + you + see)
- **Where** is the printer? (is + the printer)
- **When** does it start? (does + it + start)
- **How** can I help you? (can + I + help)
> Never: ~~What you see?~~ ~~Where the printer is?~~

### 3. A help desk call — the 6-step script
1. **Greet:** Help desk, Sara speaking. How can I help you?
2. **Name:** What's your name? What's your username?
3. **Problem:** What is the problem? When does it start?
4. **Action:** Can you restart the laptop? Please click ...
5. **Ticket:** Your ticket number is ...
6. **Close:** Is there anything else? Thank you for calling. Goodbye.

### 4. Reading a ticket table (IT tickets — fake data)
See `datasets/tickets.csv`. Example row:

| ticket_id | caller | department | short_description | priority | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| INC0010234 | Omar H. | Finance | Cannot log in to laptop | High | Open |

Read it: *The caller is Omar. He is in the Finance department. He can't log in. The priority is high. The status is open.*

## 3. Watch out! Common mistakes for Arabic speakers / انتبه

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right | Why / لماذا |
|---|---|---|
| What you see? | **What do you see?** | بعد Wh- نضع do/does قبل الفاعل. |
| Where the printer is? | **Where is the printer?** | الفعل المساعد قبل الفاعل. |
| How many user? | **How many users?** | how many + جمع. |
| When starts the error? | **When does the error start?** | does + فعل أصلي. |

## 4. 🎧 Dialogue — Full help desk call

**Sara:** Help desk, Sara speaking. How can I help you?  
**User:** Hello. I can't connect to the VPN.  
**Sara:** I'm sorry about that. What's your name and username?  
**User:** Omar Haddad. My username is o-h-a-d-d-a-d.  
**Sara:** Thank you, Omar. When does the problem start?  
**User:** It starts this morning at 8:00.  
**Sara:** What do you see on the screen?  
**User:** I see an error message: Connection failed.  
**Sara:** OK. Please restart your laptop. Don't close the ticket.  
**User:** Can you give me the ticket number?  
**Sara:** Yes. It's I-N-C zero zero one zero two three four. Is there anything else?  
**User:** No, thank you. Goodbye.  

🇸🇦 *مكالمة كاملة مع الدعم الفني: سارة تسأل عن الاسم والمشكلة والوقت ورسالة الخطأ وتعطي رقم التذكرة.*

**How to use it:** (1) Listen without reading. (2) Listen and read. (3) Read aloud with a partner (shadowing). (4) Cover one side and say your lines from memory.
