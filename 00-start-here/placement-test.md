# Placement Test (20 questions)

Questions go from very easy to harder. Stop when they become too difficult — guessing is OK but write '?'.

⏱️ Time: 20 minutes. No dictionary. Write your answers on paper, then check the answer key at the bottom.

**1.** I ___ a student.  
a) are  b) am  c) is

**2.** She ___ a manager.  
a) are  b) is  c) am

**3.** This is ___ laptop.  
a) an  b) two  c) a

**4.** Those ___ my cables.  
a) is  b) am  c) are

**5.** What time is it? 3:30  
a) Three o'clock  b) Quarter to four  c) Half past three

**6.** Sara ___ two brothers.  
a) have  b) has  c) haves

**7.** I ___ work on Friday.  
a) don't  b) not  c) doesn't

**8.** He ___ printers every day.  
a) fix  b) fixing  c) fixes

**9.** There ___ three servers.  
a) it  b) are  c) is

**10.** The laptop is ___ the desk.  
a) to  b) on  c) at

**11.** ___ you speak English?  
a) Are  b) Does  c) Can

**12.** She is ___ a report now.  
a) write  b) writes  c) writing

**13.** Yesterday I ___ to the office.  
a) gone  b) go  c) went

**14.** I ___ here since Monday.  
a) was  b) am  c) have been

**15.** If it rains, I ___ at home.  
a) stay  b) will stay  c) stayed

**16.** The server ___ by the admin last night.  
a) was restarted  b) restarts  c) restarted

**17.** She asked me ___ I had a ticket.  
a) if  b) that  c) what

**18.** I'd rather ___ the update tomorrow.  
a) to do  b) doing  c) do

**19.** Had I known, I ___ earlier.  
a) called  b) would call  c) would have called

**20.** The vulnerability, ___ was found last week, is critical.  
a) what  b) who  c) which
