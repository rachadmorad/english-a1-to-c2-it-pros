# Help Desk Survival Phrases / عبارات الدعم الفني

## Answering
- Help desk, [name] speaking. How can I help you?
- Can I have your name and username, please?
- What is the problem? / When does it start?
- What do you see on the screen?

## Asking for repetition
- Can you repeat that, please? — هل يمكنك إعادة ذلك؟
- Can you speak more slowly, please? — تكلم ببطء من فضلك
- I don't understand. / Sorry, I didn't catch that. — لم أفهم / لم أسمع جيدًا
- How do you spell that? — كيف تكتبها؟

## Giving instructions
- Please restart your laptop. — أعد تشغيل الحاسوب
- Click the Start button. / Type your password. / Press Enter.
- Don't close the window. / Don't share your password.
- Can you wait a minute, please?

## Closing
- Your ticket number is ... — رقم تذكرتك
- Is there anything else I can help you with?
- Thank you for calling. Goodbye.

## Users say
- I can't log in. / I forgot my password.
- My laptop is slow. / My screen is black.
- The printer isn't working. / I can't connect to Wi-Fi (the VPN).
- I see an error message. / I have a virus alert.
