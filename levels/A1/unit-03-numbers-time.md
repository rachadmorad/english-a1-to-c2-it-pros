# Unit 3 — Numbers, time and tickets
### الأرقام والوقت والتذاكر

> ⏱️ Time: 5–6 study sessions of 45 minutes  |  🎧 Audio script: `audio-scripts/unit-03.txt`

## 🎯 Goals / الأهداف
- Say numbers 0–100
- Read IP addresses, phone numbers and ticket numbers
- Tell the time and say days and months
- Use at / on / in for time

## 2. Lesson / الدرس

### 1. Numbers / الأرقام
| 0–10 | 11–20 | Tens |
|---|---|---|
| 0 zero (or **oh** in phone numbers) | 11 eleven | 20 twenty |
| 1 one, 2 two, 3 three | 12 twelve, 13 thirteen | 30 thirty |
| 4 four, 5 five, 6 six | 14 fourteen, 15 fifteen | 40 forty |
| 7 seven, 8 eight | 16 sixteen, 17 seventeen | 50 fifty |
| 9 nine, 10 ten | 18 eighteen, 19 nineteen | 60 sixty, 70 seventy, 80 eighty, 90 ninety |

- 21 = twenty**-one**, 45 = forty**-five**, 100 = **one hundred**.
- **Listen carefully:** thir**TEEN** (stress at the end, 13) ≠ **THIR**ty (stress at the start, 30).

### 2. Reading IT numbers
| Item | Written | Say it |
|---|---|---|
| IP address | 10.0.0.5 | "ten **dot** zero **dot** zero **dot** five" |
| IP address | 192.168.1.10 | "one ninety-two dot one sixty-eight dot one dot ten" (or digit by digit) |
| Ticket | INC0010234 | "I-N-C **zero zero** one **zero** two three four" |
| Phone (US) | 555-0142 | "five five five, **oh** one four two" |

### 3. Telling the time / الوقت
| Time | Say |
|---|---|
| 9:00 | It's **nine o'clock.** |
| 9:30 | It's **half past nine** / **nine thirty.** |
| 9:15 | It's **quarter past nine** / **nine fifteen.** |
| 9:45 | It's **quarter to ten** / **nine forty-five.** |

Ask: **What time is it?** / **What time is the meeting?** → *The meeting is **at** 9:30.*  (a.m. = صباحًا, p.m. = مساءً)

### 4. Days and months
**Days:** Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday.
**Months:** January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, December.
> Days and months start with a **capital letter** in English (unlike Arabic, which has no capitals).

### 5. at / on / in (time)
| Use | With | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **at** | clock times, *night* | at 9:00, at night |
| **on** | days, dates | on Monday, on May 5 |
| **in** | months, years, parts of the day | in June, in 2026, in the morning |

## 3. Watch out! Common mistakes for Arabic speakers / انتبه

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right | Why / لماذا |
|---|---|---|
| thirty / thirteen (same sound) | **THIR-ty vs thir-TEEN** | العشرات والـ teen يختلفان في النبر. |
| The meeting is in Monday. | **The meeting is on Monday.** | الأيام مع on. |
| monday | **Monday** | الأيام والشهور تبدأ بحرف كبير. |
| It is nine hour. | **It's nine o'clock.** | للوقت نستخدم o'clock. |

## 4. 🎧 Dialogue — Ticket on the phone

**User:** Hello, I have a problem with my laptop.  
**Sara:** Sure. What's your phone number?  
**User:** It's five five five, oh one four two.  
**Sara:** Thank you. Your ticket number is I-N-C zero zero one zero two three four.  
**User:** Can you repeat that, please?  
**Sara:** I-N-C, zero zero one, zero two three four.  
**User:** What time is the technician here?  
**Sara:** At half past three, in the afternoon.  

🇸🇦 *مستخدم يتصل بالدعم الفني. سارة تسجل رقم هاتفه وتعطيه رقم التذكرة وتخبره بموعد الفني.*

**How to use it:** (1) Listen without reading. (2) Listen and read. (3) Read aloud with a partner (shadowing). (4) Cover one side and say your lines from memory.
