## 2. Lesson / الدرس

### 1. can / can't — ability, permission, requests
| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| **Ability** (القدرة) | I **can** reset passwords. She **can't** speak Spanish. |
| **Permission** (الإذن) | **Can I** use this laptop? |
| **Request** (الطلب) | **Can you** help me? **Can you** repeat that, please? |
| **Possibility** | You **can** call the help desk 24 hours. |

### 2. Form (very easy!)
| | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| + | subject + **can** + base verb | He **can** fix it. |
| − | subject + **can't** (cannot) + base verb | I **can't** connect. |
| ? | **Can** + subject + base verb? | **Can** you help me? |
| Short | Yes, I **can**. / No, I **can't**. | |

- **No -s** for he/she/it: ~~She cans~~ → **She can**.
- **No "to"** after can: ~~I can to fix~~ → **I can fix**.
- **No do/does**: ~~Do you can help?~~ → **Can you help?**

### 3. Pronunciation
| | Sound | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| **can** (unstressed) | /kən/ (short, weak) | I **can** fix it. |
| **can't** | /kænt/ (long, clear "a") | I **can't** fix it. |
In American English, listen for the **length of the vowel**: can't is longer.

### 4. Help desk phrases (save them!)
- Can you **repeat** that, please? — هل يمكنك إعادة ذلك؟
- Can you **speak more slowly**, please?
- I **don't understand.** / Sorry, I **didn't catch** that.
- Can I **help you**? / **How can I help you?**
- Just a moment, please. / Can you **wait**, please?

## 3. Watch out! Common mistakes for Arabic speakers / انتبه

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right | Why / لماذا |
|---|---|---|
| She cans fix it. | **She can fix it.** | can لا تأخذ s. |
| I can to reset it. | **I can reset it.** | لا تستخدم to بعد can. |
| Do you can help me? | **Can you help me?** | لا نستخدم do مع can. |
| I can't to connect. | **I can't connect.** | نفس القاعدة في النفي. |

## 4. 🎧 Dialogue — Password problem

**User:** Hello. I can't log in to my account.  
**Sara:** I'm sorry to hear that. Can you tell me your username?  
**User:** Yes, it's o-m-a-r dot h.  
**Sara:** Thank you. I can reset your password now.  
**User:** Can I choose a new password?  
**Sara:** Yes, you can. But you can't use the old password.  
**User:** Sorry, can you repeat that, please?  
**Sara:** Of course. You can't use the old password.  

🇸🇦 *مستخدم لا يستطيع تسجيل الدخول. سارة تعيد ضبط كلمة المرور وتشرح له أنه لا يستطيع استخدام القديمة.*

**How to use it:** (1) Listen without reading. (2) Listen and read. (3) Read aloud with a partner (shadowing). (4) Cover one side and say your lines from memory.

## 6. Speak & Write / تحدّث واكتب

**Speak (roleplay, 3 minutes):** Partner A is the user: *I can't connect to Wi-Fi.* Partner B is the help desk: *Can I help you? Can you ...?* Use 5 help desk phrases.

**Write:** 5 sentences: *I can ... I can't ...*

## 7. Game / لعبة

**Game — Can or Can't? (true/false):** Say 10 sentences about IT: *A firewall can block attacks. A mouse can print. ...* Your partner says *True* or *False* and corrects with can/can't.
