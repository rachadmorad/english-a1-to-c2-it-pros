## 2. Lesson / الدرس

### 1. Present simple = habits and routines / المضارع البسيط
Use it for things you do **regularly** or that are **always true**.

| Pronoun | Verb (base form) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I, you, we, they | work, check, install… | I **check** my email at 8:00. |

No verb "to be" here! ~~I am check~~ → **I check**.

### 2. Negative: don't + verb
I **don't** work on Friday. / We **don't** use Windows 7. (The verb after *don't* has **no s and no -ing**.)

### 3. Questions: Do + subject + verb?
| Question | Short answer |
|---|---|
| **Do** you work at night? | Yes, I **do**. / No, I **don't**. |
| **Do** they use a ticket system? | Yes, they **do**. |
| **Where do** you work? | I work at a hospital. |
| **What time do** you start? | I start at 8:30. |

### 4. Time words
**at** 8:00, **on** Monday, **every day**, **in the morning**, **from** 9 **to** 5.

### 5. Adverbs of frequency / ظروف التكرار
always (100%) → usually → often → sometimes → rarely → never (0%)
- **Before** the verb: I **always** lock my laptop. We **usually** start at 8.
- **After** *am/is/are*: I am **always** on time.
- *Never* is already negative: I **never** share my password. (Not ~~I don't never~~)

### 6. Useful IT verbs
log in / log out, restart, update, install, reset, back up, scan, connect.

## 3. Watch out! Common mistakes for Arabic speakers / انتبه

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right | Why / لماذا |
|---|---|---|
| I am work at a bank. | **I work at a bank.** | لا تضع am مع فعل آخر. |
| I go to the work. | **I go to work.** | go to work بدون the. |
| I don't never share it. | **I never share it.** | never سالبة أصلًا. |
| I start at the 8. | **I start at 8:00.** | الأرقام بدون the. |

## 4. 🎧 Dialogue — A busy morning

**Sara:** What time do you start work, Omar?  
**Omar:** I start at 8:00. I always check the servers first.  
**Sara:** Do you answer calls too?  
**Omar:** No, I don't. The help desk answers calls.  
**Sara:** Do you work on Friday?  
**Omar:** Yes, I do. But I finish early.  
**Sara:** Where do you have lunch?  
**Omar:** I usually have lunch in the office.  

🇸🇦 *سارة تسأل عمر عن روتينه اليومي: يبدأ في 8:00 ويفحص الخوادم ولا يردّ على المكالمات.*

**How to use it:** (1) Listen without reading. (2) Listen and read. (3) Read aloud with a partner (shadowing). (4) Cover one side and say your lines from memory.

**Write:** A short paragraph (5 sentences) about your day.

## 7. Game / لعبة

**Game — Mime and guess:** Act a verb (*restart, read, write, answer, log in*). Your friend says: *Do you ___?* — *Yes, I do!* / *No, I don't.*

## 8. Mini-quiz / اختبار قصير (8 questions — target 7/8)

**1.** I ___ my email every morning.  
a) checking  b) am check  c) check

**2.** We ___ work on Sunday.  
a) aren't  b) don't  c) doesn't

**3.** ___ you start at 8:00?  
a) Does  b) Do  c) Are

**4.** Where ___ they work?  
a) do  b) does  c) are

**5.** I ___ share my password. (0%)  
a) always  b) never  c) usually

**6.** Which is correct?  
a) I go to the work.  b) I go to work.  c) I go work.

**7.** 'Do you use Linux?' — Yes,  
a) I use.  b) I do.  c) I am.

**8.** Where does 'usually' go?  
a) I usually check.  b) I check usually.  c) Usually I am check.
