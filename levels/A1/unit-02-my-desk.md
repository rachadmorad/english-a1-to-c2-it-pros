# Unit 2 — This is my desk
### هذا مكتبي

> ⏱️ Time: 5–6 study sessions of 45 minutes  |  🎧 Audio script: `audio-scripts/unit-02.txt`

## 🎯 Goals / الأهداف
- Name common IT objects

## 2. Lesson / الدرس

### 1. Plural nouns / الجمع
| Rule | Singular → Plural |
|---|---|
| most nouns: + **s** | cable → cable**s**, laptop → laptop**s** |
| ends in s, x, ch, sh: + **es** | box → box**es**, switch → switch**es** |
| consonant + y → **ies** | battery → batter**ies** |
| irregular | mouse → **mice**, person → **people** |

### 2. this / that / these / those (أسماء الإشارة)
| | Near (قريب) | Far (بعيد) |
|---|---|---|
| **One** thing | **This** is my laptop. | **That** is the server. |
| **Many** things | **These** are my cables. | **Those** are the printers. |

### 3. *to be* — full table
| | Affirmative | Negative | Question |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | I am | I am not | Am I? |
| you / we / they | you **are** | you **aren't** | **Are** you? |
| he / she / it | it **is** | it **isn't** | **Is** it? |

- **it** = a thing or animal: *It is a router.* Short answers: **Yes, it is. / No, it isn't. / Yes, they are.**

### 4. Adjectives come BEFORE the noun
Arabic: laptop new (لابتوب جديد). English: **a new laptop**. Adjectives **never** take -s: *two new laptops* (not ~~news laptops~~).

### 5. a / an with objects
**a** mouse, **a** printer, **an** old server, **a** USB drive (USB starts with the sound "you" = a consonant sound).

## 3. Watch out! Common mistakes for Arabic speakers / انتبه

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right | Why / لماذا |
|---|---|---|
| a laptop new | **a new laptop** | الصفة قبل الاسم. |
| This are my cables. | **These are my cables.** | cables جمع → these. |
| two news laptops | **two new laptops** | الصفة لا تُجمع. |
| It is a broken. | **It is broken.** | بدون a قبل الصفة وحدها. |

## 4. 🎧 Dialogue — In the supply room

**Omar:** Sara, is this your laptop?  
**Sara:** No, it isn't. That laptop is mine. It's the new one.  
**Omar:** OK. Are these your cables?  
**Sara:** No, they aren't. Those cables are Ali's.  
**Omar:** What is that?  
**Sara:** It's a router. It's broken.  
**Omar:** Are those keyboards new?  
**Sara:** Yes, they are.  

🇸🇦 *عمر وسارة في غرفة المخزن يسألان عن ملكية الأجهزة والكابلات. الراوتر معطّل.*

**How to use it:** (1) Listen without reading. (2) Listen and read. (3) Read aloud with a partner (shadowing). (4) Cover one side and say your lines from memory.

**Write:** 5 sentences about your desk. Use *new, old, broken*.

## 7. Game / لعبة

**Game — I Spy IT:**
```
          ┌──────────────┐
          │ 🖥️ MONITOR   │
          └──────┬───────┘
  🖱️ mouse   ⌨️ keyboard    📱 phone
  💾 USB drive        🎧 headset
```
Look at the picture. A friend says: *"I spy a small thing. It's near the keyboard."* You ask: *"Is it a mouse?"* Play 5 rounds.
