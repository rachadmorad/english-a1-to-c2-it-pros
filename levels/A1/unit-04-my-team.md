## 2. Lesson / الدرس

### 1. have / has (American English)
| Pronoun | Verb | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I, you, we, they | **have** | I **have** a laptop. They **have** two servers. |
| he, she, it | **has** | She **has** a new badge. The server **has** 32 GB. |

- **Negative:** I **don't have** a badge. (For he/she: **doesn't have** — see Unit 6.)
- **Question:** **Do** you **have** a laptop? — Yes, I **do**. / No, I **don't**.
- In American English use **have** (not *have got*): *I have an account.*

### 2. Possessive adjectives / صفات الملكية
| Pronoun | Possessive | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I | **my** | my laptop |
| you | **your** | your password |
| he | **his** | his badge |
| she | **her** | her account |
| we | **our** | our team |
| they | **their** | their office |
| it | **its** | its IP address |

> **Important:** *his/her* depend on the **owner**, not on the object. Sara's laptop → **her** laptop. Omar's keyboards → **his** keyboards.

### 3. Possessive 's
**Omar's** laptop = the laptop of Omar. **The manager's** office. (Not ~~the laptop of Omar~~ in everyday English.)

### 4. Age / العمر
Arabic: عمري ٣٠. English: **I am 30** (years old). ~~I have 30 years~~.
Ask: **How old are you?** — *I'm 30.*  |  **How old is the server?** — *It's five years old.*

## 3. Watch out! Common mistakes for Arabic speakers / انتبه

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right | Why / لماذا |
|---|---|---|
| I have 30 years. | **I am 30 (years old).** | العمر مع to be وليس have. |
| He have a badge. | **He has a badge.** | he/she/it → has. |
| Sara has his laptop. | **Sara has her laptop.** | الملكية حسب المالك: Sara = her. |
| the laptop of Omar | **Omar's laptop** | استخدم 's بدل of مع الأشخاص. |

## 4. 🎧 Dialogue — First day: badge and account

**Ali:** Welcome to the team, Sara. Do you have your badge?  
**Sara:** No, I don't. I don't have a badge yet.  
**Ali:** Here is your badge. And this is your laptop.  
**Sara:** Thank you. Do I have an email address?  
**Ali:** Yes, you do. It's sara at corp dot example.  
**Sara:** Is Omar your colleague?  
**Ali:** Yes, he is. His office is next to my office.  
**Sara:** How old is this laptop?  
**Ali:** It's two years old.  

🇸🇦 *علي يرحب بسارة ويعطيها بطاقة الدخول والحاسوب والبريد الإلكتروني، ويعرّفها على مكتب عمر.*

**How to use it:** (1) Listen without reading. (2) Listen and read. (3) Read aloud with a partner (shadowing). (4) Cover one side and say your lines from memory.

## 6. Speak & Write / تحدّث واكتب

**Speak:** Say five sentences about your family or your team: *My brother has ... Our team has ... His name is ...*

**Write:** Draw your team tree:
```
            [ IT Manager: ______ ]
              /        |       \
     [ Engineer ] [ Technician ] [ You ]
```
Write one sentence for each person: *Ali is the manager. He has ...*

## 7. Game / لعبة

**Game — Who has it? (cards):** Write 8 cards: *badge, laptop, account, license, headset, phone, USB drive, password.* Give each to a person (real or imaginary). Ask a friend: *Who has the badge?* — *Omar has it. It's his badge.*

## 8. Mini-quiz / اختبار قصير (8 questions — target 7/8)

**1.** She ___ a new laptop.  
a) have  b) has  c) haves

**2.** I ___ 30 years old.  
a) have  b) is  c) am

**3.** Sara has ___ badge. (owner = Sara)  
a) his  b) she  c) her

**4.** Omar has a laptop. It's ___ laptop.  
a) its  b) her  c) his

**5.** Which is correct?  
a) laptop of Ali's  b) Ali's laptop  c) Ali laptop's

**6.** 'Do you have an account?' — No,  
a) I haven't.  b) I not.  c) I don't.

**7.** We ___ two servers.  
a) have  b) has  c) having

**8.** 'How old is the server?'  
a) It has 5 years.  b) It's 5 years old.  c) It is 5 year.
