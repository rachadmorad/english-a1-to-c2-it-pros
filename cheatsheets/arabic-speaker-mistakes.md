# Top 15 Mistakes for Arabic Speakers / أشهر الأخطاء

| # | ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | I engineer. | I **am** an engineer. | The verb *to be* is required |
| 2 | She a manager. | She **is** a manager. | same |
| 3 | I am work here. | I work here. | Don't put *am* before another verb |
| 4 | a laptop new | a **new laptop** | Adjective before noun |
| 5 | two news laptops | two **new** laptops | Adjectives have no plural |
| 6 | He have a badge. | He **has** a badge. | he/she/it → has |
| 7 | She work here. | She work**s** here. | he/she/it → verb + s |
| 8 | He doesn't works. | He doesn't **work**. | Base verb after doesn't |
| 9 | Does he works? | Does he **work**? | Base verb after does |
| 10 | I have 30 years. | I **am** 30. | Age uses *be* |
| 11 | I go to the work. | I go to work. | Fixed expression |
| 12 | Where the printer is? | Where **is the printer**? | Auxiliary before subject |
| 13 | She cans fix it. | She **can** fix it. | can never takes -s |
| 14 | Do you can help? | **Can** you help? | No *do* with can |
| 15 | In the room a server. | There **is** a server in the room. | Use there is/are |

**Sound mistakes:** P→B (*parking* = "barking"), V→F (*very* = "fairy"), short vowels (ship/sheep), adding a vowel to clusters (*street* = "istreet"). See `pronunciation-guide.md`.
