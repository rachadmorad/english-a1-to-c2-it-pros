# 🎲 Help Desk Adventure (board game)
**Players:** 1–4 (or alone vs the clock) · **Needs:** a die (or a random number app), a pencil per player

Move from START to FINISH. When you land on a square, do the challenge **in English, out loud**. If you fail, go back 2 squares.

```
START → 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 6
                              ↓
FINISH ← 20 ← 19 ← 18 ← 17 ← 7
  (…)                        ↓
 12 ← 13 ← 14 ← 15 ← 16     8 → 9 → 10 → 11 ↑
```
(Squares 7–16 go clockwise around the board: draw it on paper.)

| # | Challenge |
|---|---|
| 1 | Say hello and introduce yourself (name, job, country). |
| 2 | Spell your email address with *at* and *dot*. |
| 3 | Name 5 things on a desk. |
| 4 | Say the time now and the day today. |
| 5 | Read aloud: `192.168.1.10` |
| 6 | Tell me 3 things you **have** in your bag. |
| 7 | Say 3 sentences about your routine (*I always ...*). |
| 8 | Ask me 2 questions with **Do you...?** |
| 9 | Describe a friend with **he/she + verb-s** (3 sentences). |
| 10 | Say where 3 things are in the room (*on, under, next to*). |
| 11 | Ask for help politely (**Can you ...?**). Then say what you **can't** do. |
| 12 | Say what is happening now (**is/are + -ing**) — 3 sentences. |
| 13 | Give 3 instructions (*Click..., Don't...*). |
| 14 | Ask 3 Wh- questions (what, where, when). |
| 15 | Read the error: *Connection failed.* and say what it means in Arabic. |
| 16 | Do the 6-step help desk greeting (from Unit 10). |
| 17 | Spell **firewall**, **password**, **router**. |
| 18 | Say 5 IT verbs. |
| 19 | Read ticket INC0010234 aloud (see `datasets/tickets.csv`). |
| 20 | 🏁 FINISH: say 5 sentences about your job in English! |
