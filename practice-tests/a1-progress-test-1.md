# A1 Progress Test 1 — Units 1 to 5

Pass mark: 20/25 (80%). If lower, review the units listed next to each answer.

⏱️ Time: 30 minutes. No dictionary. Write your answers on paper, then check the answer key at the bottom.

**1.** Good ___! (8:00 a.m.)  
a) morning  b) evening  c) night

**2.** I ___ a network engineer.  
a) am  b) is  c) are

**3.** Ali ___ the manager.  
a) are  b) am  c) is

**4.** ___ IT technician  
a) a  b) the  c) an

**5.** 'Is she from Egypt?' — No,  
a) she isn't.  b) she doesn't.  c) she not.

**6.** Plural of 'battery':  
a) batteryes  b) batteries  c) batterys

**7.** ___ are my cables. (near)  
a) That  b) This  c) These

**8.** She has a ___ laptop.  
a) laptop new  b) new  c) news

**9.** The mouse ___ broken.  
a) are  b) am  c) is

**10.** Twelve + eight =  
a) twenty  b) eighty  c) eighteen

**11.** How do you say 10.0.0.1?  
a) ten dot zero dot zero dot one  b) ten and zero  c) ten point zero

**12.** The meeting is ___ Tuesday.  
a) at  b) on  c) in

**13.** The meeting is ___ 9:15.  
a) on  b) at  c) in

**14.** 9:45 =  
a) half past nine  b) quarter past nine  c) quarter to ten

**15.** He ___ a new badge.  
a) have  b) has  c) is

**16.** Sara has a laptop. It's ___ laptop.  
a) her  b) she  c) his

**17.** I ___ 30 years old.  
a) am  b) is  c) have

**18.** The laptop of Ali =  
a) Alis laptop  b) Ali's laptop  c) Ali laptop

**19.** We ___ start at 8:00.  
a) is  b) are  c) — (no verb)

**20.** I ___ work on Friday.  
a) isn't  b) doesn't  c) don't

**21.** ___ you answer calls?  
a) Are  b) Does  c) Do

**22.** I ___ share my password. (0%)  
a) usually  b) never  c) always

**23.** Which is correct?  
a) I go to work.  b) I go to the work.  c) I go at work.

**24.** 'Do you have a badge?' — Yes,  
a) I am.  b) I do.  c) I have.

**25.** Correct: 'I have 25 years.'  
a) I has 25.  b) I am 25 (years old).  c) I am have 25.
